Contact Our Team ↗
Is my appointment confirmed after submitting the form?

No. Submitting the request sends your details to the salon through WhatsApp. Your appointment is confirmed only after salon staff replies.

Can I request a specific appointment time?

Yes. Select your preferred time slot in the form. The team will confirm whether that time is available.

Can I book bridal and groom services?

Yes. Choose bridal makeup, bridal hairstyle or groom styling from the service list and share your event requirements.

Can I book for more than one person?

Yes. Select the number of people and mention any additional requirements in the message field.

Can I change my appointment request?

Contact the salon team directly to discuss a different date or time. Any change is subject to availability.
